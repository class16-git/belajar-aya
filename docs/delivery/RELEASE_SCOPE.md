# Release scope statement (audit ATL-07)

Atlas is released as an **honestly limited family pilot**, not as a complete ages-2 to Grade 6 curriculum.
The objective registry (120 objectives) is a planning map. Its counts are internal coverage, not a percentage of a
validated full curriculum.

## What production offers (`/api/health` → `visible`)
| Stage | What a family gets | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Age 2–3 (Adek)** | Printable caregiver cards and parent observations. No screen time. | Released (owner-reviewed) |
| **Age 3–4, 4–5** | Printable caregiver cards (Montessori-inspired practical life, sensorial, language, maths, early STEM) | Released, except one card withheld for re-review (ATL-10) |
| **Kindergarten** | Caregiver cards; on-screen maths: teen numbers, bonds to 20, picture word problems; Grade 1 skills when ready | Released (K lessons owner-approved); linkage card withheld for re-review |
| **Grade 1 (Kakak)** | On-screen maths: counting to 20, bonds to 10, add/sub within 10 (CPA), bar-model stories. Robotics: trace and debug. Cards. | Released |
| **Reading, all stages** | Reading journeys; 12 external Book Dash titles (links only, parent PIN gate); original comprehension passages | Released; 2 passages withheld for re-review (ATL-08/09) |
| **Grades 2–6** | Reading journeys and passages only | **Not released for maths, Montessori or robotics.** The learn page says "coming soon". |
| **Islamic studies** | Nothing | **Not released.** Needs a named qualified scholar and translation reviewer (ADR-0010). |

## Promises Atlas does not make
- **Completeness:** no claim of a complete or accredited curriculum. Montessori and Singapore-style maths are
  descriptions of the teaching approach only.
- **Islamic content:** nothing is shown until a qualified religious review is recorded.
- **Book licences:** Book Dash titles are linked, not hosted, because title-level licences are unconfirmed.
- **AI:** no child-facing AI. Grading and recommendations are deterministic.

## Rule for unsupported areas
A grade or domain with no reviewed learning journey shows a "coming soon" state. It never appears complete. Adding
breadth comes after one coherent sequence is finished. The next planned sequence is Grade 1 completion (see
FINAL_HANDOVER §5).
