# Curriculum framework

## Educational framing and fidelity
"North American Montessori" is not one universal, licensed syllabus. Map against chosen primary sources (e.g. AMI/AMS-aligned materials and the child's school's documented scope and sequence) only after obtaining and verifying them. "Singapore Math" likewise refers to pedagogical approaches and various curricula; do not claim formal alignment with Singapore MOE or commercial programs without a reviewed crosswalk. Build original materials.

## Age bands
| Band | Approx age | Core Montessori domains | Mathematics emphasis | Experience |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Toddler | 2–3 | Practical life, language, sensorial, movement | Quantity awareness 1–3, sorting, one-to-one | Parent-led offline |
| Children's House / Preschool | 3–4 | Practical life, sensorial, language, cultural | Numerals, quantities, classification, patterns | Concrete/offline first |
| Preschool / Pre-K | 4–5 | Language, sensorial, cultural, math | Counting 0–20, comparison, compositions | Manipulatives then pictures |
| Kindergarten | 5–6 | Early literacy, math, geography, science | Number bonds to 10, addition/subtraction, shapes | CPA progression |
| Elementary G1 | 6–7 | Math, language, cultural, practical life | Number sense to 100, operations, measurement, geometry | CPA + problem solving |
| Elementary G2–G3 | 7–9 | Interdisciplinary projects | Place value, multiplication/division, fractions, measurement | Models and reasoning |
| Elementary G4–G6 | 9–12 | Research, cultural studies, advanced math | Fractions, decimals, ratios, geometry, data | Transfer and independent inquiry |

These bands are approximate, not rigid age-based prescriptions. Skill readiness and educator/parent judgment govern selection.

## Domain taxonomy
- Mathematics: number sense, operations, place value, fractions, measurement, geometry, data, problem solving.
- Language: oral language, phonemic awareness, phonics, reading comprehension, writing.
- Practical life: care of self, care of environment, coordination, grace/courtesy.
- Sensorial: visual, tactile, auditory, classification, sequencing.
- Cultural: geography, science/nature, history, arts, community.
- Executive function: attention, planning, persistence, self-regulation (observational, non-diagnostic).

## Singapore Math teaching requirements
- Every relevant math skill carries `cpa_stage`: concrete, pictorial, abstract.
- Use number bonds for part-whole reasoning, ten-frames for quantity, bar models for comparative/word problems, and multiple strategies where appropriate.
- Never advance solely from answer speed. Require independent correct answers across varied item forms and a delayed check.
- Distinguish computation errors, place-value errors, representation misunderstandings and reading-language difficulties.

## Montessori teaching requirements
- Offer child choice among a small curated set; preserve repetition without pressure.
- Prefer self-correcting materials and observable outcomes.
- Respect uninterrupted work and avoid unnecessary celebratory animations or interruptions.
- For toddler activities, include materials, preparation, demonstration, safety supervision, observation cues and cleanup.
- Avoid representing a digital simulation as a replacement for tactile materials.

## Starter sequence: toddler
| Skill ID | Goal | Parent-led example | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOD-SORT-COLOR | Sort two distinct colors | Large safe blocks into bowls | Avoid small choking hazards |
| TOD-MATCH-OBJECT | Match identical objects | Pair large household items | Supervise closely |
| TOD-LANG-NAME | Name familiar objects | Three-period lesson with objects | Accept gestures and home language |
| TOD-QUANTITY-3 | Explore groups of 1–3 | Count large toy animals | Do not force numerals |
| TOD-PRACT-POUR | Practice coordination | Pour dry large items or water | Assess spill and choking risks |

## Lesson object contract
Each lesson must define: id, title (id/en), domain, age band, prerequisites, objective, materials, modality (offline/interactive), CPA stage when relevant, task generator, correct-answer evaluator, misconception tags, hint ladder, parent notes, safety notes, accessibility, content version and review status.

## Expansion coverage planning
- Preschool: 100+ original activity blueprints across five domains, with review before release.
- KG: core literacy and foundational numeracy progression, integrated offline work.
- G1–G6: year-by-year domain map and prerequisite graph; author depth iteratively, not by bulk unreviewed AI generation.
- For each level publish coverage dashboard: planned, drafted, reviewed, validated, released.

## Assessment
Use low-stakes formative evidence, not clinical or standardized diagnostic claims. A mastery flag requires variety (at least 3 independent correct items across at least 2 formats, plus delayed retention check as configured). Parent observations may supplement but should not silently override math evidence.
