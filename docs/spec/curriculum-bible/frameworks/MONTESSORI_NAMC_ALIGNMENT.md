# Montessori / NAMC alignment strategy

NAMC (North American Montessori Center) is a specific provider of Montessori training/curriculum materials. Atlas should be **Montessori-informed and mapped against lawfully available or licensed NAMC references**, not claim to reproduce NAMC lesson albums or hold NAMC certification.

## Strands
- Practical Life: care of self, environment, grace/courtesy, movement and coordination.
- Sensorial: visual, tactile, auditory, olfactory and dimensional discrimination.
- Language: oral vocabulary, phonological awareness, decoding, writing, reading comprehension.
- Mathematics: quantity before symbol, decimal system, operations, fractions, geometry.
- Cultural/Cosmic: geography, botany, zoology, history, science, peace and interconnectedness.

## Lesson design: three-period lesson
1. Name/show concept with physical material.
2. Recognize/select through play and observation.
3. Recall/name when developmentally ready.

## Required mapping fields
`strand`, `sub_strand`, `montessori_material`, `control_of_error`, `prerequisites`, `physical_activity`, `teacher_observation`, `namc_reference_status` (unverified/licensed/educator_mapped), `copyright_clearance`.

Never copy proprietary album text, worksheets, photographs or assessment items without permission. Use original analogues and licensed materials where applicable.
