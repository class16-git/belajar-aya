# Islamic Studies: ages 2 through Grade 6

## Requested orientation
The family's requested orientation is **Ahl al-Sunnah wa al-Jama'ah**, with **Ash'ari and Maturidi** theology and particular interest in the scholarship associated with **Shaykh Abdullah al-Harari**. This is a family-selected doctrinal curriculum, not a claim that all Sunni traditions use identical formulations. Teach respectfully and avoid denigrating other schools or people.

### Foundational theological topic
**الله موجود بلا مكان — Allah mawjud bila makan**: “Allah exists without [being confined to] a place.” In the requested Ash'ari/Maturidi-oriented presentation, Allah is not a body and is not spatially contained like created things. Exact formulations, supporting textual proofs and age-appropriate analogies must be approved by a qualified scholar familiar with the requested tradition. Avoid imagery depicting Allah or analogies that equate Allah with creation.

## Strands
1. Aqidah: knowing Allah, divine transcendence, attributes, prophets, faith, evidence and respectful questions.
2. Qur'an: listening, pronunciation/tajwid, short surahs, meanings, recitation ethics and memorization without pressure.
3. Worship/fiqh: taharah, wudu, salah, fasting, zakat, pilgrimage, gradually; label the selected fiqh school where rulings differ.
4. Seerah and prophetic history: kindness, honesty, courage, mercy, community and chronology.
5. Adab/akhlaq: family respect, truthfulness, gratitude, generosity, environmental stewardship, responsible digital conduct.
6. Arabic: letters/sounds, everyday vocabulary, comprehension, short texts and source literacy.

## Example age-sensitive lessons
- **Toddler:** after watering a plant, caregiver says a simple gratitude phrase; child may repeat or observe. No quizzes about metaphysics.
- **Grade 1:** compare created objects that occupy space; teacher explains in carefully reviewed wording that Allah is not like created objects. Do **not** infer doctrine from a simplistic physics analogy.
- **Grade 4:** discussion card on why Creator/creation distinction matters; invite questions without shaming.
- **Grade 6:** guided source study of a scholar-approved passage on divine transcendence, with original Arabic, translation, provenance, interpretation and respectful discussion.

## Required source approval
Every aqidah lesson requires `doctrinal_school`, `arabic_source`, `edition`, `page_or_section`, `translation_reviewer`, `scholar_reviewer`, `approval_status`. Do not fabricate citations, chains of transmission, hadith grades or quotations.
