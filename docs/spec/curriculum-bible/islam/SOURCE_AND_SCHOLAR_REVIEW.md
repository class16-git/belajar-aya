# Islamic reference registry and verification protocol

## User-specified sources and traditions
- Ahl al-Sunnah wa al-Jama'ah (family's selected orientation).
- Ash'ariyyah and Maturidiyyah (theological traditions; use school-specific nuance where needed).
- Shaykh Abdullah al-Harari (attribution must be tied to a verified edition/primary source).
- “Allah mawjud bila makan” (doctrinal formulation; precise sourcing and pedagogy to be scholar reviewed).
- **Al-Qawl al-Jaliyy / al-Qawl al-Jaliy**: title supplied by user; **exact Arabic title, author, edition, subject and authoritative text have not been verified in this package**. Do not assert that it contains any particular passage until a qualified reviewer supplies and verifies the exact reference. This may be a transliteration ambiguity.

## Review procedure
1. Identify exact Arabic title, author, publisher, edition, date, page and rights status.
2. Capture exact passage only when licensed/permitted; preserve Arabic diacritics if available.
3. Have qualified reviewer validate attribution, translation and doctrinal interpretation.
4. Tag school-specific statements; distinguish broadly shared Islamic teachings from tradition-specific explanations.
5. Check child suitability, emotional tone, visual depiction, accessibility and non-sectarian respect.
6. Store approval evidence and version. Re-review after edits.

## Prohibited AI behavior
Never fabricate Qur'an verses, hadith, scholarly quotes, book citations or doctrinal consensus. Do not let generative AI independently issue fatwas, assess children's faith, or answer open-ended child theological questions without a parent/educator-approved response set. Use a safe fallback: “Let's ask your parent or teacher together.”
