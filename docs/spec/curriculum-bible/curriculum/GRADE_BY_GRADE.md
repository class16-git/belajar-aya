# Grade-by-grade syllabus outlines

Each stage below is a **scope**, not a complete lesson bank. All activities require original lesson authoring, teacher review, and local adaptation.

## Age 2–3

**Montessori / general studies:** Practical life: carry tray, spoon transfer, tidy up; sensorial: big/small, colors, texture; language: object naming and rhymes; culture: family, plants, animals.

**Singapore Math:** Match sets 1–3; compare more/less with objects; sort by one property.

**Robotics/STEM:** Build towers; predict rolling/falling; sequence first/next; no screens required.

**Islamic studies:** Bismillah before eating, alhamdulillah for gratitude, greetings, kindness, short recitation exposure.

**Assessment:** Adult observes pointing, matching and independent attempts; no scored exams.

## Age 3–4

**Montessori / general studies:** Pouring, dressing frames, sound discrimination, prewriting, three-period lessons.

**Singapore Math:** Count and match up to 10; AB patterns; basic 2D shapes; compare lengths.

**Robotics/STEM:** Unplugged arrows, simple paths, balance and ramps.

**Islamic studies:** Cleanliness, sharing, family adab, listening to short stories, memorization by choice.

**Assessment:** Observation checklist and photo-free notes.

## Age 4–5

**Montessori / general studies:** Phoneme-to-letter connections, movable letters, quantities/symbols, continents and life cycles.

**Singapore Math:** Compose/decompose within 10; number bonds; addition/subtraction with counters.

**Robotics/STEM:** Sequence and debug simple robot-like actions; design-build-test.

**Islamic studies:** Intro to five pillars, wudu demonstration, selected short surahs and duas.

**Assessment:** Hands-on demonstrations and parent-confirmed practice.

## Age 5–6 / Kindergarten

**Montessori / general studies:** Reading simple words, place-value bead equivalents, maps, botany, time routines.

**Singapore Math:** Bonds to 20; ten-frames; tens and ones; pictorial word problems.

**Robotics/STEM:** Loops as repeated actions, simple mechanical linkages, supervised programmable toys.

**Islamic studies:** Prayer movements and meaning, prophetic stories, compassion and responsibility.

**Assessment:** Portfolio and readiness observation; no forced acceleration.

## Grade 1

**Montessori / general studies:** Reading fluency, writing, decimal system, science observation, geography.

**Singapore Math:** Numbers to 100; addition/subtraction to 20 then 100; lengths, time, money, shapes; CPA.

**Robotics/STEM:** Directional commands, debug path, simple sensors and engineering notebooks.

**Islamic studies:** Foundational creed with age-appropriate language, daily duas, wudu, salah, short surahs, adab.

**Assessment:** Short independent practice plus manipulatives; review after 1/7/30 days.

## Grade 2

**Montessori / general studies:** Grammar, timelines, place-value operations, ecology and research.

**Singapore Math:** Numbers to 1,000; regrouping; multiplication as equal groups; division sharing; simple fractions.

**Robotics/STEM:** Block coding sequences, loops, gears and levers, test criteria.

**Islamic studies:** Prayer consistency without coercion, seerah chronology, truthful speech, basic aqidah terms.

**Assessment:** Explain with objects, pictures and words.

## Grade 3

**Montessori / general studies:** Cosmic education, ecology, fractions, independent reading and reports.

**Singapore Math:** Times tables, division, fractions, 2-step bar models, perimeter and area.

**Robotics/STEM:** If/then, simple sensors, prototypes, error logs.

**Islamic studies:** Distinguish Creator and creation in approved language; worship, ethics, Qur’an recitation.

**Assessment:** Reasoning rubrics and project demonstrations.

## Grade 4

**Montessori / general studies:** History/geography research, experiments, writing and geometry.

**Singapore Math:** Multi-digit operations, equivalent fractions, decimals, measurement conversions, area.

**Robotics/STEM:** Low-voltage circuits, motor control, variables and data collection.

**Islamic studies:** Aqidah with carefully sourced texts, fiqh practice, civic manners and charity.

**Assessment:** Problem-solving portfolios and source-aware reflection.

## Grade 5

**Montessori / general studies:** Scientific method, biology, world history, research presentations.

**Singapore Math:** Fractions/decimals, percentage, ratio foundations, volume and multi-step problems.

**Robotics/STEM:** Microcontrollers, loops, conditionals, debugging and data ethics.

**Islamic studies:** Intro to rational/textual reasoning in creed with scholar review, worship and service.

**Assessment:** Independent inquiry with mentor feedback.

## Grade 6

**Montessori / general studies:** Interdisciplinary capstones, advanced grammar, civic responsibility, ecology.

**Singapore Math:** Ratios, percentages, algebraic patterns, geometry and data literacy.

**Robotics/STEM:** Robotics capstone with sensors, testing, documentation and safety review.

**Islamic studies:** Age-appropriate Ash’ari/Maturidi vocabulary, verified source reading, jurisprudential respect and service.

**Assessment:** Capstone exhibition and mastery evidence.
