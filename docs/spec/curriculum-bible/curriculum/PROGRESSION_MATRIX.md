# Cross-disciplinary progression matrix

| Stage | Montessori emphasis | Singapore Math | Robotics/STEM | Islamic education | Delivery mode |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2–3 | practical life, matching, naming, sensorial contrasts | sort, compare, one-to-one correspondence, quantities 1–3 then 1–5 as ready | cause/effect, sequence two actions, build and sort | love, gratitude, simple adab, short duas heard with family | mostly physical, adult-led |
| 3–4 | pouring, dressing, sound games, sandpaper-like tactile symbols | count to 10, compare, patterns, shapes | directional words, 2–3-step unplugged sequences | greetings, cleanliness, kindness, basic Arabic words | physical-first |
| 4–5 | phonemic awareness, movable alphabet equivalents, number rods | number bonds within 10, simple addition/subtraction with objects | simple algorithms, mazes, gears, building | daily manners, short surahs with accurate recitation, stories | blended |
| 5–6 / K | early reading, decimal materials, geography | bonds to 20, tens/ones, basic word problems | loops through repeated actions, simple robots with supervision | pillars at introductory level, prayer observation, respect and responsibility | blended |
| Grade 1 | reading fluency, place value, nature observation | add/subtract within 20/100, place value, measurement, shapes | directional robot, debugging, sensors as observation | basic creed in child-safe wording, wudu and salah introduction, Qur'an and adab | short guided + independent |
| Grade 2 | research, grammar, timelines, materials | add/subtract within 1,000, multiplication/division models | block coding, mechanisms, design journal | daily practice, short seerah, aqidah foundations | guided projects |
| Grade 3 | cosmic stories, fractions, ecology | multiplication/division, fractions, bar models | sensors, conditionals, iterative engineering | worship literacy, prophetic character, theological vocabulary carefully introduced | projects |
| Grade 4 | independent inquiry, geography, history | multi-step problems, fractions/decimals, area | circuits (low voltage), robotics systems, data | creed reasoning with concrete analogies, fiqh and ethics | research + build |
| Grade 5 | advanced research, scientific method | ratios introduction, decimals, percentages, volume | microcontrollers, data logging, algorithms | textual evidence literacy, distinctions of Creator/creation, history | project portfolio |
| Grade 6 | synthesis, service, interdisciplinary capstone | ratios, percentages, algebra readiness, geometry | robotics capstone, programming, design ethics | mature age-appropriate aqidah, worship, adab, service, source verification | independent with mentors |

This is an original planning map, not a verbatim NAMC or Singapore syllabus. Sequence by readiness and local school objectives; validate grade alignment with licensed source materials and teachers.
