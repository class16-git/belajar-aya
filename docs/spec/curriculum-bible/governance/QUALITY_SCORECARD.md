# Atlas Curriculum QA scorecard

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Learning effectiveness | 20 | mastery, delayed recall, transfer, observation |
| Curriculum accuracy & sequencing | 20 | educator-reviewed objectives, prerequisites, item proofs |
| Age/development fit | 15 | usability, attention, motor and reading suitability |
| Montessori/CPA/robotics fidelity | 15 | physical-to-symbolic progression, real-world transfer |
| Child safety/privacy | 15 | isolation, consent, logs, content gates |
| UX/accessibility/bilingual quality | 10 | tablet/phone, WCAG where applicable, audio, Arabic/ID/EN QA |
| Parent transparency | 5 | explainable progress and approvals |

Threshold suggestion: >=90/100 **and** no critical/high safety, privacy, content-correctness or unapproved-publication issues. Scores are provisional design targets; not proof of learning effectiveness. Religious accuracy is a hard approval gate, not a score that can be compensated by attractive UI.
