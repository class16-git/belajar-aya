# Educational, safety and editorial guardrails

- **Age ≠ grade ≠ mastery:** date of birth informs motor/language/safety affordances, enrolled grade is an editable school setting, and actual placement comes from observed mastery. Never auto-promote grade based on birthday.
- Use the learner's configured IANA timezone for age; recompute on local date change and profile load. Birthdays must not be embedded in public fixtures or analytics.
- Ages 2–3: caregiver-led, brief optional activities, large targets, audio-first, no compulsory screen time. For ages 2–5 prioritize real-world manipulatives and off-screen practice.
- No punitive streaks, pressure loops, leaderboards, ads, tracking pixels or open-ended child AI chat.
- No child-to-child data access, parent unlock required, no real child data in repository, tests, prompts, logs or third-party AI requests.
- Correctness before engagement: no ambiguous answer keys, misleading mastery or unsourced doctrinal assertions.
- Respect different developmental trajectories and accessibility needs; avoid presenting skill delays as deficits.
- Islamic learning must be gentle, non-coercive, respectful of other people and free of sectarian hostility. Age-appropriate moral formation and practice come before abstract theological controversy.
- Separate **draft**, **subject-matter reviewed**, **religious reviewed** (when applicable), **parent approved**, and **published** states. Only approved content may appear in production.
- Require scholarly/qualified educator review of doctrinal claims, Arabic wording, hadith authenticity, fiqh positions, and any attributed quotations.
- Human approval is not delegable to Claude Code/Codex; AI can recommend, draft, test and flag issues, but cannot self-certify pedagogical or theological authority.
