Real World Mission

Feel the Sound

Put your hand gently on your throat and say "aaah". First say it low, then say it high.

Can you feel the buzzing change? A fast buzz makes a high sound. A slow buzz makes a low sound. Your voice is a string!

Parent Panel

This simulation connects pitch to vibration speed: short strings wiggle faster and sound higher; long strings wiggle slower and sound lower. The wave drawing shows frequency; the Gentle/Normal/Hard buttons show amplitude (volume).
