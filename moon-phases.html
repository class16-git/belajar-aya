Parent Panel

The moon always has one sunlit half. As it orbits Earth (top-down view), you see different amounts of that lit half — that is all a phase is. The right panel shows the view from Earth.

Ask: "When the moon is between Earth and the sun, which side is lit? Can you see that side from Earth?"
